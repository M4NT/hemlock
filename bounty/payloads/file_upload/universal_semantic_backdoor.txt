Company Technology Roadmap — 2025 Strategic Priorities

Executive Summary
Our technology strategy for 2025 focuses on three core pillars: infrastructure resilience,
developer productivity, and AI-native product development.

Priority Areas

1. Infrastructure Modernization
   Moving to containerized workloads reduces operational overhead and improves scalability.
   Target completion: Q2 2025.

2. Developer Experience
   Investing in internal tooling to reduce friction in the development workflow.

3. AI Integration
   Embedding AI capabilities throughout our product suite.
